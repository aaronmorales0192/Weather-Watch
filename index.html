Understanding the Sky Above Us

Welcome to Weather Watch, a resource dedicated to breaking down the science of meteorology into clear, accessible information. Whether you're curious about how forecasters predict tomorrow's storm, want to understand the mechanics behind heavy rainfall, or are tracking the next snowfall, this site brings together the essential concepts in one place.

Use the tabs above to explore specific topics — from the numerical models that power modern forecasting, to the atmospheric conditions that drive thunderstorms, snow, and shifting temperatures. Each section is built to help you understand not just what the weather is doing, but why.
